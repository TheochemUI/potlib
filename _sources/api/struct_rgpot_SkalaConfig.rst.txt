Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Kohn-Sham DFT through libnwchemc, with Skala as the XC keyword.

Skala is not a standalone PES. Energy and forces come from nwchemc_energy_gradient after NWChemParams.dft.xc is set.
